How the math works

Each sound draws every dial from a bell curve: center (your setting, shifted by the feeling and depth, pulled by any active label) and spread. The dark band is 68% of sounds, light band 95%.

Like or Nope is a reward of +1 or −1 minus a running average. Each unlocked dial's center moves toward sounds that scored well, and its spread shrinks once good sounds agree (a small reinforcement-learning rule called REINFORCE with a baseline).

A label's meaning is the average of the sounds tagged + for it, pulled toward neutral until there is enough evidence, and weighted more where the examples agree and differ from the − sounds. Its impact bar on each dial reflects how far that dial's average sits from neutral.

Nothing to label yet

Tap a word once for yes, again for no, a third time to clear. This teaches the label what it means for this voice.

Learned meanings

Each card is one generated sound. The wavy line is its melody (pitch over time): ● a sung note, ● a drum hit, a gap in the line is a rest. Bigger dots are louder notes.
